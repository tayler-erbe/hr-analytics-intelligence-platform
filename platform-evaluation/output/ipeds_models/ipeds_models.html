What national IPEDS patterns predict, and what actually happened

Institution-level models trained on 3,462 degree-granting four-year institutions (Fall 2015–2023, 25,211 institution-years), validated forward in time, then used to score the University of Illinois campuses leave-institution-out.

Read first. These are public, self-reported institution aggregates, not payroll records. Year-to-year moves mix real change with reporting and classification changes (2018 SOC recoding, hospital/branch scope, management recoding). Models describe association, not cause. A residual says a campus moved differently from institutions that looked like it; it does not say why.
