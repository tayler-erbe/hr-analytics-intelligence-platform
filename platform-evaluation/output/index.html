Workforce Evidence Portal

Every offline demo page in this workspace, with its data class and its main caveat. All pages open from disk with no network calls. Read the full project map in ARCHITECTURE.md.

No university employee data appears on any page. Public pages use published open data (O*NET, IPEDS, QCEW, QWI, the SUCSS catalog, the Anthropic Economic Index). Synthetic pages use invented or generated records. Nothing here scores an individual or forecasts job loss.

Demo pages

  • External workforce evidence

    Publiclake/external_evidence.html

    Peer pay, higher-ed employment, Illinois turnover flows and task-level AI-usage evidence from the lake's gold views.

    Caveat: the peer panel is a proposal, not an approved HR peer group. Women-to-men pay ratios are unadjusted and are not a pay-equity finding.

  • Workforce graph explorer

    Publicgraph/workforce_graph_explorer.html

    The role ontology: SUCSS classes, O*NET occupations, tasks, skills, technology, institutions and labor-market facts, with provenance on every link.

    Caveat: SUCSS-to-O*NET links are unreviewed lexical title suggestions, not mappings.

  • AI reskilling explorer

    Publicreskilling/role_evolution_reskilling.html

    For each role: which tasks show observed AI use, which skills hold up, adjacent roles with skill gaps, and AI-literacy targets.

    Caveat: role grain only. Observed AI usage is evidence about tasks, not a job-loss forecast. SUCSS mappings are unreviewed.

  • Workforce scenario planner

    Syntheticworkforce_sim/scenario_planner.html

    Monte Carlo scenarios over five years (retirement waves, hiring freezes, reskilling, AI capacity) with P10/P50/P90 ranges.

    Caveat: synthetic population calibrated to UIUC's public IPEDS total (12,905) and QWI hazards. Not a prediction about UIUC staff; the AI lever is an assumption.

  • Workforce Analytics Intelligence Platform: delivery dashboard

    Syntheticwap_prototype/wap_dashboard.html

    The ESGC work-request prototype end to end on the Cookbook-shaped EDW layout: the five success-measure views (promotion expectations by role and unit, succession risk, internal mobility, growth forecasts, proactive indicators), model quality and data-quality provenance, from the Power BI star schema.

    Caveat: synthetic workforce; on the 813-person set most quarterly department cells are suppressed at k=10 (a UIUC-scale rebuild is in progress). Model A published default is the base-rate-by-step expectation because no learner passed the pre-registered thresholds.

  • Integrated workforce data network

    Syntheticintegration/integration_explorer.html

    Codex's EDW-shaped sample workforce joined to every public source: class to SUCSS, O*NET, SOC, BLS wages and projections, AI exposure, job and class evolution; org to campus, IPEDS, QCEW and QWI.

    Caveat: internal data is synthetic and not calibrated to EDW; SUCSS-to-O*NET links are unreviewed; campus assignment is synthetic.

  • Succession planning models

    Syntheticsuccession_models/succession_planning.html

    EDW-ready vacancy-risk hazard models (6/12/24 months), career-transition matrix, bench strength and a disabled-by-default readiness ranking with fairness audit.

    Caveat: validated on synthetic EDW-shaped data only; run on real data only on an approved machine after sign-off. Role/unit outputs, not people rankings.

  • Turnover and retirement-wave forecasts

    Publicturnover_forecast/turnover_forecast.html

    Real Census QWI for Illinois and 13 peer states: 8-quarter forecasts of separations, 55+ separations and hires, backtested against seasonal naive; Illinois vs peers.

    Caveat: public and private colleges combined; gains over naive are modest and 80% intervals under-cover.

  • National IPEDS predictive models

    Publicipeds_models/ipeds_models.html

    Models trained on 3,462 institutions (2015–2023) score U of I campuses: expected vs actual tenure, new hires, staffing and real pay; data-driven peer groups.

    Caveat: institution-level aggregates; only new-hire rates are clearly predictable; intervals under-cover after 2021–22.

  • Job evolution from O*NET history

    Publicjob_evolution/job_evolution_explorer.html

    20 O*NET releases (2008–2026): task churn, skill drift, technology adoption and a role-family watchlist for U of I.

    Caveat: O*NET update timing drives most change; the task-change model is modest and poorly calibrated.

  • Illinois civil-service class evolution

    Publicclass_evolution/class_evolution_explorer.html

    Caveat: revision prediction did not beat spec age; shown as a descriptive index. Unreviewed O*NET links.

Ground rules

  • Only public data is kept in Git. Internal HR records stay on approved machines.
  • No individual scoring. Outputs are at role, unit or cohort level (Illinois HB 3773).
  • Restricted text goes only to local or enterprise-approved models.
  • No model grades another model. Scores come from exact comparison or human review.
