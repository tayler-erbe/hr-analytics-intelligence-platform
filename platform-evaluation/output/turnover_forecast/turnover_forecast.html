Illinois higher-ed turnover & retirement-wave forecast

Census LEHD Quarterly Workforce Indicators, NAICS 6113 (colleges, universities and professional schools), Illinois and 13 peer states. Real published data; forecasts are statistical, with honest rolling-origin backtests.

Read before using.
  • QWI covers all ownerships: public and private colleges are combined. It is not the University of Illinois System.
  • Published quarters are revised in each release; backtests use today's vintage (pseudo-out-of-sample, not real-time). Real QWI arrives ~3 quarters late.
  • The latest quarters are partial: 2025Q4 separations and 2025Q3–Q4 turnover are not yet published; 2025Q2–Q3 values are provisional.
  • Age 55+ separations are a proxy for retirement: QWI does not record why a job ended.
