Sponsored funding cliff: University of Illinois (UIUC and UIC)

Public federal award data as of . The view is aggregate only: by agency, institute and NIH department category, with no investigators shown. It addresses gap G3: positions funded by grants and soft money.

Read this first. These figures cover awards, not people. An obligation is not money spent, and money spent is not salary. An award that expires is often renewed, extended at no cost, or replaced. "Ending" means the recorded end date falls in that quarter. It does not mean the money or the job is lost. Subawards the University receives from other institutions are not included. The number of positions at risk can only be estimated by joining Banner labor distribution to grant end dates. That join is designed below but not run.

From award risk to position risk (designed, not populated)

Banner HR labor distribution (NBRJLBD, or EDW T_PAYR_ACCTG_DETL) records what share of each position's pay is charged to each FOAPAL fund. Banner Finance links each fund to a grant (FTVFUND → FRBGRNT), and the grant carries its project end, its budget end and the sponsor's award number. That award number is the same FAIN or core project number used on this page. Joined as of a date, the result reads: in department D, N positions have at least 50% of their pay on grants ending in quarter Q, adding up to F funded FTE and $S of salary. Cells with fewer than 5 positions are suppressed. The SQL is in sponsored_funding/hr_join_design.sql and is tested only on synthetic rows.
