Trend-to-reality nowcast

Do public job-posting trends tell us anything about what then happens to Illinois higher-ed employment, hires and separations? Real public data only: Indeed Hiring Lab posting indices, BLS QCEW and CES, Census QWI, and the Anthropic Economic Index.

What the data say

    Indeed indices measure the change in posting volume since 1 Feb 2020 (=100), seasonally adjusted. They are not vacancy counts. Higher ed is not an Indeed sector; "Education & Instruction" includes K–12 and private tutoring. Only about 5½ years of postings overlap the outcome data, and the 2020–21 COVID swing dominates any full-sample statistic.

    Lead-lag: do postings move first?

    Outcomes are transformed to quarterly log changes with quarter-of-year effects removed. Postings, already seasonally adjusted, are transformed to quarterly log changes. Cross-correlations are prewhitened: an AR(1) is fitted to the posting series and applied to both series. Positive lag k means postings lead by k quarters. Cells outside the ±1.96/√n band are outlined. The Granger F-tests regress the outcome on a constant, quarter dummies, its own lags and posting lags. P-values are adjusted by Benjamini–Hochberg within each sample.

    Nowcast and two-quarter forecast

    Direct h-step models of year-over-year log growth, backtested with rolling origins. Each origin trains only on quarters it could have seen, with at least 8 training rows from 2021Q1. Postings for the target quarter count as known because Indeed publishes daily, while QCEW and QWI arrive 5–9 months late. The seasonal naive model repeats the same quarter last year. CES (monthly BLS payroll survey) is a hard-data benchmark, not a posting signal. QWI quarters marked provisional are excluded from scoring.

    Horizon

    RMSE and MAE are in log points of YoY growth (≈ % error in the level). Skill = 1 − MSEmodel/MSEreference. Positive means better. DM is the Diebold–Mariano test with the Harvey small-sample correction. With about 10 scored quarters, p-values are fragile. Robustness column: RMSE when training starts at 2022Q1 (scored 2023Q3 onward).

    Occupation context: national demand next to observed AI usage

    For each Indeed sector, the related SOC occupations come from an analyst mapping built on Indeed's example titles. It is unreviewed. AI usage comes from the Anthropic Economic Index (observed exposure, labor_market_impacts release; conversation share, 2025-03-27 release). This is a structural snapshot, not a time series. The panel is correlational and descriptive: it says nothing about cause, and many sectors had a remote-work hiring boom and bust in the same years.

    Caveats and provenance

    • Indeed indices are not vacancy counts. They track posting volume relative to 1 Feb 2020 on one job site, with Indeed's own seasonal adjustment (revised in Nov 2024). Indeed publishes no state × sector series, so every sector signal is national.
    • Higher ed is not an Indeed sector. Campus jobs are spread across Education & Instruction, Administrative Assistance, Accounting, HR, IT, Research and others. The postings in those sectors are mostly from other employers.
    • Short history. Postings start in Feb 2020. There are 22–24 quarterly changes, and backtests score 9–12 quarters. Low power cuts both ways: a null result is not proof of no relationship.
    • COVID break. 2020–21 dominates variances and correlations. Every lead-lag test is also run on a post-COVID sample, divergence fits exclude 2020Q2–2021Q2, and the nowcast shows a post-2022 training robustness check.
    • Coverage. QWI is all ownerships (public and private together). QCEW suppresses Illinois state-university cells for 2025 and every local-government cell. CES Illinois covers private junior colleges plus colleges (NAICS 6112–6113).
    • Revisions. QWI's latest two quarters are provisional. CES is benchmarked each year. QCEW revises. Backtests use today's vintages, not real-time vintages, which flatters every model a little.
    • AI usage is evidence, not prediction (lake contract rule 10). No job-loss score is computed.
