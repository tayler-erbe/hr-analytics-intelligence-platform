University of Illinois vs Big Ten peers, Fall 2015-2023

Public IPEDS human-resources data: staffing, faculty tenure, new hires and pay, with the U of I campuses drawn against the Big Ten peer median and middle half.

Read with care. These are institution-level aggregates that institutions report to IPEDS. They are not payroll records, and differences often reflect how a campus classifies jobs or which units (hospitals, branch campuses) it reports. Women-to-men ratios are raw ratios of averages and are not a pay-equity finding. The peer group is a proposed Big Ten panel, not an approved HR comparison group.

Comparability

  • Occupation categories (2018 SOC). From the 2018-19 collection (Fall 2018), IPEDS assigns jobs to its occupation groups using the 2018 Standard Occupational Classification instead of the 2010 one. Group codes did not change. Nationally, no group shows a discontinuity at 2018 larger than its normal year-to-year movement (table below), but individual institutions recoded staff in 2018-2022. Those level shifts are flagged; the dashed line marks the 2018 change.
  • Faculty pay, 2015. The 2015-16 salary file has no 9-month-equated average, so 2015 uses 9 times the average weighted monthly salary. In 2016, when both exist, the two methods differ by a median of about half a percent at rank level.
  • Penn State reported Fall 2019 HR for the whole university, including its Commonwealth campuses, and has no usable Fall 2020-2021 HR records. Those years are left out of charts and peer bands. Peer medians use 14 peers in those years.
  • Staff per student. Fall staff headcount is divided by 12-month FTE students for the same academic year (EFIA of the following year). A fall-FTE version is offered as a check. System Offices enrol no students, so they have no ratio.
  • New hires are full-time permanent hires from November 1 of the prior year to October 31, per 100 full-time staff on November 1. Fall 2015 used a July-October window (4 months), so it is not charted. Hires are not net of departures, so this is not a turnover rate.
  • Imputation. No panel value in these series was imputed by NCES. The NCES item flags are kept in the Parquet tables.
