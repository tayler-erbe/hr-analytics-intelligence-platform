About this toolkit

Purpose. Evidence of applied AI proficiency for the staff AI PD tiers (AI Explorer → AI Catalyst → AI Practitioner), measured from work samples rather than attendance.

Who scores. Trained human raters. No model grades another model. Software computes agreement (weighted kappa, Krippendorff's alpha, ICC), rater severity and drift, item statistics, Angoff cut scores, decision consistency and DIF.

Privacy. Pseudonymous codes only; work samples and scores stay on approved university systems; program reporting uses aggregates with cells under 10 suppressed. This page makes no network requests (a Content-Security-Policy blocks them).

Limits. Badge thresholds are provisional until a real Angoff panel meets. The report shows how the toolkit behaves on synthetic data with planted properties; it says nothing about real staff or raters.
