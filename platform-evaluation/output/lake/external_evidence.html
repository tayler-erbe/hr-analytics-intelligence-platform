External workforce evidence

Public data on the University of Illinois System's labor market, peers and roles. Built 2026-11-06 from the public HR data lake (IPEDS Fall 2023, BLS QCEW 2015–2024, Census LEHD QWI through 2025, O*NET 31.0, Anthropic Economic Index). It contains no university employee records. This page gives context for questions, not findings about U of I staff.

Peer pay: full-time non-instructional staff

Average salary = salary outlays ÷ full-time staff, by IPEDS occupation group, Fall 2023. Groups mix very different roles, and cost of living differs by campus, so treat gaps as questions to investigate. The peer panel is a proposal, not an approved HR peer group. Source: IPEDS SAL2023_NIS.

Faculty pay by rank, with the raw women-to-men ratio

Nine-month-equated average salary for full-time instructional staff. The ratio is unadjusted: it does not control for discipline, years in rank or appointment, and is not a pay-equity finding. Source: IPEDS SAL2023_IS.

Observed AI usage in administrative and IT occupations

Anthropic Economic Index "observed exposure": the share of an occupation's tasks seen in Claude usage. It measures where AI is being used, among a selected population of users. It is not a forecast of job loss and says nothing about U of I staff. Use it to decide where to look at task redesign and training. Job zone 1–5 is O*NET preparation level.
