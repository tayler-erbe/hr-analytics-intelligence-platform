Bench strength for critical role families

Coverage = expected internal moves into the critical tier from feeder roles ÷ expected exits that leave the tier, over 24 months. Bars are P10–P90 across Monte Carlo draws of the transition matrix (Dirichlet posterior) and of individual events; dots are the median. Coverage below 1 means the tier is expected to need external hires. It is a talent-pool flow, not a named slate.

How to read this page

  • Vacancy risk counts separations and internal moves out of a position. It is not an individual flight-risk score, and position-level scores are kept in a restricted folder because a one-incumbent position's score describes its incumbent.
  • Age enters the vacancy model only through assumption-labelled SURS eligibility bands. The no-age ablation in the backtest table shows what those features add.
  • The critical-role list, role families, SURS rules and pay ranges in this synthetic run are assumptions to be replaced by HR-owned definitions on the approved machine (see run_real.md).
