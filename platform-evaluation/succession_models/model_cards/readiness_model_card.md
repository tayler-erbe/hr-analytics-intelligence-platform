## Governance gate

## Intended use (if ever approved)
Advisory input to a **human-led talent-pool discussion** for a critical role family: which feeder-role incumbents might be
invited to development opportunities. Not a slate, not a promotion or selection decision, not a substitute for posting or
civil-service register rules. Under HB 3773 promotion and selection for training are covered decisions: notice, human
decision-maker, logging of AI suggestion vs final decision, and periodic adverse-impact review are required.

## Model
LambdaMART (LightGBM `lambdarank`) with a logistic-regression comparator and a random baseline. Query = (origin, critical
family); candidates = incumbents of roles with >= 2% estimated transition probability into the family; label = moved into
the family within 24 months.
Features: role tenure, service, level gap, same-family flag, transition probability, class move rate, pay position,
part-time flag, prior internal move. Excluded: age, birth date, SURS status, sex, race/ethnicity, ZIP and other listed proxies.
Service and role tenure are **age-correlated proxies**; they are kept only so the audit can test them (age 40+ group).

## Why this is risky
The label is historical movement. If past promotions were unequal, the ranking reproduces that pattern through correlated
features even without protected attributes (see the synthetic demonstration). A readiness score is not evidence of
readiness against defined role challenges, which is the practice the research recommends over 9-box "potential".
