# Model card: position vacancy risk (6 / 12 / 24 months)

> Generated by `run_all.py` on 2026-11-06 from the **synthetic** run. On synthetic data every number describes the synthetic generator, not the University.

## Intended use
Workforce-planning input at **position, role and unit level**: where vacancies are likely, so units can plan
knowledge transfer, overlap hires and talent-pool development. Published outputs are role- and unit-level
aggregates with n >= 10.

## Out of scope (prohibited)
- Any decision about the current incumbent of a position (assignment, pay, discipline, discharge, encouragement to retire,
  exclusion from development). A single-incumbent position's score is effectively a statement about that person, so
  position-level output is **restricted** (approved machine, named HR planning staff, not shown on dashboards).
- Use as an individual "flight-risk" score (HB 3773; ADEA for age-derived inputs).

## Model
- Primary: discrete-time **cause-specific logistic hazards** (separation; internal move out of the position) on 6-month
  incumbent person-periods; P(vacancy within h) = 1 - prod(1 - h_sep - h_move) with age, service and tenure rolled forward.
- Comparators: **Cox PH** and **Weibull AFT** (lifelines) on annual landmark spells; baselines: role-family base rate,
  age-band-only rule; an ablation without age-derived features; on synthetic data an oracle using the true parameters.
- Calibration: per-horizon logistic recalibration fitted out-of-time on the origin 24 months earlier.

## Features
`tenure_lt2`, `surs_unreduced`, `surs_reduced_only`, `surs_within_3y`, `part_time`, `pay_pos_c`, `unit_churn_c`, `recent_change`, `in_role_ge2`, `top_level`, role-family indicators.
Age enters only through SURS-eligibility bands (`surs_unreduced`, `surs_reduced_only`, `surs_within_3y`): ASSUMPTION: simplified SURS Tier 1/Tier 2 age+service rules, service = years since first hire; verify with SURS before any use.
Excluded: sex, race/ethnicity, ZIP code / address / commute, names, leave usage, graduation year, performance ratings.

## Parameter recovery (synthetic only)

35 of 40 planted hazard coefficients fall inside the fitted 95% CI; mean absolute error 0.084 on the 6-month logit scale. Full table: `export/parameter_recovery.csv`.

## Known limitations
- Discrimination is modest (AUC ~0.6) even for the oracle: most exits are not predictable from administrative data. Use
  aggregated expected counts, not rankings.
- Training labels are as known at the origin; late-keyed exits are missed in training (small downward bias).
- Unit churn is a proxy for unobserved unit-level conditions; the SURS rule is simplified.
- Real EDW validation, subgroup calibration by protected group and Counsel review are still required (run_real.md).
