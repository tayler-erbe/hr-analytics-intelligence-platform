Class Specifications

The University System office is required to maintain written class specifications, as approved by the Merit Board, for each class in the classification plan. Each specification includes the class title, the function of the position, characteristic duties and responsibilities, minimum acceptable qualifications, including any special licenses or certificates required by state or federal laws, and additional desirable qualifications.

To view the entire list of classifications, click on the Show All button. You can also narrow your search by clicking on the Quick Links buttons or by entering criteria into the search field.
